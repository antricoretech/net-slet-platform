# Content Track C0.B — Licensing and Provenance Rules

Rights are fail-closed. The supported bases are original internal content, licensed third-party content, official permission, public domain, open licence, research-only, rights unknown, restricted and disputed.

Each use is independently permissioned: research, student practice, strict mock, translation, adaptation, download and redistribution. A reviewed legal basis, reference and current status are required. Research-only, unknown, restricted and disputed content cannot satisfy publication or strict-mock eligibility. Expired and revoked evidence is blocked.

`sourceEventId` and a canonical text input are required for content lineage. These are audit identifiers, not a claim that matching text is plagiarism.
