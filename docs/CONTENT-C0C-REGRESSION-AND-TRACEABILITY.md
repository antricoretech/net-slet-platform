# Content Track C0.C — Regression and Traceability

The local harness runs ExamDNA validation, C0.B eligibility, deterministic form generation, scoring-policy validation, manifest construction and governance decision without database access. A controlled seed and fixed qualification timestamp make fixture runs reproducible.

The traceability matrix retains all six pattern families: the candidate family is represented as under-test, while other families remain unresolved engineering classifications. This prevents a passing fixture from being reported as official fidelity.

Regression evidence must cover paper/section counts, language, groups, attempt ceilings, scoring determinism, answer-key lineage, student-safe projections, duplicate exposure and manifest reproducibility.
