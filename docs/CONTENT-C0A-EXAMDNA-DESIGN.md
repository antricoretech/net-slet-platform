# Content Track C0.A — ExamDNA design

ExamDNA separates examination identity, cycle, subject, family and official-source evidence. A reusable family is only a compatibility aid; it is never proof that a named examination follows that family.

Official readiness requires exact-scope verified source evidence, SHA-256 artifact identity, authorized reviewer metadata, approval state, matching rule hash, regression evidence and sufficient trusted question inventory. Historical, secondary or incomplete records remain `RESEARCH_ONLY`/`TO_VERIFY`.

The six engineering families are represented as research-only compatibility assessments: continuous dual CBT, CSIR parted CBT, single/staged OMR, independent OMR papers, Kerala-specific exception and per-cycle exception/pending verification. Official rules always override family defaults.
