# Content Track C0.C — Publication Governance

Governance decisions are `READY`, `CONDITIONAL` or `BLOCKED`, and always return `strictOfficialPatternPublicationAuthorized: false`. `READY` means the evaluated technical and evidence gates are complete, not that publication has occurred. Separate administrative authorization and an audit event remain required.

`CONDITIONAL` covers incomplete or synthetic/non-authoritative evidence and never authorizes strict official-pattern publication. `BLOCKED` identifies failed requirements and remediation.

Required separation of duties covers content owner, SME, examination-rule owner and rights reviewer. Publication authorization is a separate role. Challenges quarantine affected versions; answer-key corrections create a new key and authorized rescoring review; emergency withdrawal stops future exposure while preserving historical attempts and evidence.
